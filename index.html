How it works

Four steps from your first glass to a lasting habit.

  1. Set your target

    Choose a daily amount in Settings, or let the calculator suggest one from your weight and activity.

  2. Log each serving

    Tap a cup, glass, mug or bottle on the Tracker, or type any amount. Made a mistake? Undo it.

  3. Watch it fill

    The carafe rises with every serving and changes colour when you reach your target.

  4. Review your progress

    See every day on the Calendar, and your streaks, charts and achievements on the Dashboard.

Hydration tips

Simple habits that make it easier to drink enough.

How much water do I need?

Needs vary with body size, activity, climate and health. Many guidelines suggest about 2 to 2.7 litres of fluid a day for adult women and 2.5 to 3.7 litres for adult men, counting water from all drinks and food. Treat any target as a starting point and adjust to how you feel.

Tie drinking to your routine

Have a glass when you wake up, with each meal, and when you sit down to study or work. Habits linked to things you already do are easier to keep.

Keep water within reach

A refillable bottle on your desk or in your bag makes it much more likely you'll sip through the day.

Drink more in heat and during exercise

You lose extra water through sweat on hot days and when you're active. Drink before, during and after exercise.

Check the signs

Thirst, dark yellow urine, headaches, tiredness and a dry mouth can mean you need more fluid. Pale yellow urine usually means you're drinking enough.

Don't overdo it

Drinking very large amounts in a short time can be harmful. Spread your intake across the day. If you have a heart, kidney or other health condition, ask a health professional what target suits you.
